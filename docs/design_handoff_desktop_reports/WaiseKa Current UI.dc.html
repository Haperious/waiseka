The 50-30-20 Rule

Tatlong Hati ng Sweldo

Sweldo just came in? Before it disappears into bills, Shopee carts, and 'panggastos lang,' try splitting it into three.

  • 50% needs — rent, bills, groceries, transpo
  • 30% wants — Jollibee, barkada nights, subscriptions
  • 20% savings — move it the day sweldo lands

Build Your Emergency Fund First

Hiwalay na Pondo Para sa 'Kung Sakali'

Most people save whatever's left after rent, bills, Jollibee, and that 12.12 sale na hindi naman kailangan. Kadalasan, walang natitira.

  • Start with ₱10,000 — one month of the basics
  • Keep it in a separate bank you don't carry a card for
  • Build to 3–6 months of expenses over time
