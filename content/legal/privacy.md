### 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account details | Name, email address, birthdate, password (stored only as a one-way hash); for users under 18, a parent or guardian's name, email and consent record | You, at sign-up |

**What we never collect:** your online banking or e-wallet username, password, OTP or PIN; full card numbers or CVV. WaiseKa does not connect to your bank and cannot move money. Please do not type full account or card numbers into notes or account names.

> **Sa madaling salita:** Ang kinukuha lang namin ay ang info na ikaw mismo ang naglagay - account mo, transactions, budgets, goals. Hindi namin hinihingi at hindi namin kailanman kukunin ang password o OTP ng bank o GCash mo.

### 5. Smart Import and uploaded files

When you upload a statement or screenshot, our server extracts the text, turns it into draft transactions for you to review, and then deletes the file. We keep only the transactions you choose to save. Uploaded files are not sent to any third party, not used to train any model, and not kept in backups.

> **Sa madaling salita:** Pagka-upload mo ng statement, binabasa lang namin para gawing transactions, tapos binubura agad ang file.

### 8. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your data is processed (this policy)
- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data - most of it you can edit directly in the app
- **Erasure or blocking** - ask us to delete your account and data
- **Object** to processing, including withdrawing consent for notifications or Smart Import
- **Data portability** - get your data in a common machine-readable format (CSV or JSON)
- **Damages** for harm caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or unauthorized use of your data
- **File a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph)

**How to use them:** email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account, with the subject "Privacy request". We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete the request within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Account deletion from Settings is coming; until then, email us.

> **Sa madaling salita:** Data mo 'yan, ikaw ang boss. Pwede mong makita, i-download, ayusin o ipabura. Email lang sa amin at aasikasuhin namin within 30 days.

### 9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, per-user data isolation, and restricted administrative access. Details are on our [Security page](/security). No system is perfectly secure, so we also commit to telling you if something goes wrong.

### 10. Data breaches

If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to cause you real harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of discovering it. We will tell you what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing about it, and what you can do to protect yourself.

> **Sa madaling salita:** Kung magkaproblema sa security, sasabihan ka namin agad - within 72 hours. Hindi namin itatago.

### 11. Cookies and local storage

We use only what the app needs to work: a secure session cookie that keeps you logged in, and browser storage for display settings such as light or dark mode. We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required first.

### 12. Age requirement

WaiseKa is for people aged 16 and over. We ask for your birthdate at sign-up only to confirm eligibility. If you are 16 or 17, we email your parent or legal guardian to ask for their consent; until they confirm, your account stays in a limited mode: {{LIMITED_MODE}}. A guardian can ask us to access, correct or delete the minor's data through the contact details below. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16 and will delete such accounts when we learn of them.

> **Sa madaling salita:** Pwede ka na mag-WaiseKa from 16! Kung 16 o 17 ka pa lang, kailangan lang ng OK ng parent o guardian mo.

### 13. Changes to this policy

We will post any update on this page with a new effective date. For material changes - such as a new type of data, a new provider that sees your financial records, or new AI features - we will notify you by email or in the app at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
